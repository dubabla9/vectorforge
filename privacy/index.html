Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VectorForge handles information when you use our website and browser-based image conversion tools.

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

VectorForge is a browser-based toolkit that provides image conversion and file preparation tools. We respect your privacy and aim to keep our services simple and transparent.

By using the VectorForge website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Images and Files You Upload

VectorForge tools are designed to process images directly in your web browser whenever the tool supports browser-based processing.

Files selected for conversion are used to perform the requested operation, such as generating SVG, EPS, preview or ZIP files.

VectorForge does not require you to create an account in order to use the core conversion tools.

Important: You should avoid uploading confidential, private or sensitive files to any online service unless you are comfortable using that service.

3. Information We Collect

VectorForge does not require users to provide personal information such as a name, phone number or account password to use the core image conversion tools.

Like most websites, technical information may be made available to the hosting or infrastructure providers needed to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser information, device information, request information and basic server logs.

The exact information collected by third-party infrastructure may depend on their own systems and policies.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

VectorForge may use cookies or similar technologies if required by website functionality, hosting, security, analytics or future services.

If third-party services are added to VectorForge, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

VectorForge may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to host, secure, operate or improve the website.

Third-party providers may process limited technical information as necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its systems operating securely. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should take appropriate precautions when handling sensitive or confidential files.

7. Children's Privacy

VectorForge is a general-purpose online tool website and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the core conversion tools.

8. External Links

VectorForge may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites that we do not operate.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VectorForge's features, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or VectorForge's privacy practices, please use the contact information provided on our Contact Us page.
